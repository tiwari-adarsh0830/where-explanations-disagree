Do groups of heads behave like their parts?

Each bar is the drop in logit(IO) − logit(S) when a whole group of heads is patched with its corrupted output at the same time, with a 95% bootstrap interval over prompts. The tick is what you would predict by adding up the effects of patching each head on its own. A positive value means the model gets worse at the task. The three panels split the prompts by name order; the one matching the Name order filter is outlined.
